Profile

About Adv. Aroon Parsad

Aroon Parsad was enrolled as an advocate on 13 August 2010 and today practises as an Advocate of the High Court of Sindh. From his chamber at Paradise Chamber, Saddar, he conducts a busy practice in criminal, civil, family and constitutional matters, appearing regularly before the Sindh High Court and the courts at City Court, Karachi.

His second vocation is teaching. Qanoon Ki Baat, the legal awareness programme he founded, has been running on YouTube since March 2012 — 266 videos on court procedure, cross examination, bail, family law, cyber crime and the everyday problems of students and young lawyers, explained the way he would explain them across his own desk. The Express Tribune has covered his work for student rights and against harassment; he writes and speaks on education, and is described on his own profiles as a social activist and legal advisor.

The Bar has trusted him with office. He was elected Treasurer of the Karachi Bar Association for 2017, sits on the Education Committee of the Sindh Bar Council, chairs the Young Lawyers Forum Pakistan, serves as Chief Patron of Qanoonkibaat, and is President of The Shelter, an NGO. Every year he gives a month of that experience back, mentoring law students in the Qanoonkibaat Internship Program at City Court.

Areas of Practice

Every Client Matters

Clear advice, honest guidance and strong representation — in the areas of law where families and businesses need it most.

Criminal Law & Bail

Bail applications, trials and appeals before the Sindh High Court and the trial courts, including matters under the Pakistan Penal Code such as cheque dishonour (Section 489-F).

Civil Litigation

Civil suits, injunctions and recovery matters, from the legal notice and plaint through evidence to final arguments.

Family Law

Petitions before the Sindh High Court for the enforcement of fundamental rights guaranteed by the Constitution of Pakistan.

Cases under the PECA law, online harassment, deepfake material and defamation, including suits for damages.

Harassment, ragging and education disputes affecting students — an area of work he has pursued in court and in public for many years.

Qanoonkibaat Internship Program

The Qanoonkibaat Internship Program is a one-month, court-based internship for law students and fresh graduates, held at City Court, Karachi (Jinnah Auditorium) with the Young Lawyers Forum Pakistan. It is deliberately practical: interns learn by drafting real documents and watching real courts work.

  • Drafting: FIRs, applications under Sections 22-A and 22-B, bail before arrest petitions and legal notices
  • Court visits and observation of proceedings
  • Moot exercises and sessions with practising advocates
  • Certificate on completion, signed by Adv. Aroon Parsad and Adv. Shatidevi Parsad

Ask About the Next Batch

A Note to Students

The program runs in batches through the year. Places are limited and allotted on application. Students from all law colleges in Karachi are welcome to apply; bring your enrollment proof when you visit the chamber.
